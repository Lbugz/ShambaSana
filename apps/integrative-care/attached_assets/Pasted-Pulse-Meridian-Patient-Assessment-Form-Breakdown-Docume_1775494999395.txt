Pulse Meridian — Patient Assessment Form Breakdown
Document Type: Product Reference
Application: Pulse Meridian Clinical Reader
Version: Current
Date: April 2026

Overview
The Pulse Meridian assessment form is a 6-step intake process designed to collect comprehensive patient data for use in generating AI-powered TCM diagnostic reports. The form is available in two modes:

Public Mode — Patients complete it online before their visit; submission goes into a pending queue for practitioner review.
Admin/Practitioner Mode — Practitioners complete it in real-time during an office visit.
Both modes collect identical data.

Form Structure at a Glance
Step	Name	Purpose
1	Patient Information	Identity and contact details
2	Current Health & Symptoms	How the patient feels today
3	Medical History	Past conditions, medications, allergies
4	Vitals & Physical Data	Measurable clinical data
5	TCM Assessment	Tongue exam, pulse, constitution
6	Lifestyle & Harmony	Diet, movement, stress, life balance
Step 1 — Patient Information
Title: "Tell us about yourself"
Purpose: Establish patient identity and ensure results can be delivered.

Field	Type	Required	Notes
Full Name	Text input	Yes	Validated on entry
Age	Number input	No	Range: 1–120
Gender Identity	Dropdown	No	Male / Female / Non-binary / Prefer not to say
Phone Number	Tel input	No	For appointment reminders
Email Address	Email input	Yes	Report delivery address; format validated
Assessment Date	Date picker	Yes	Auto-filled with today's date
Step 2 — Current Health & Symptoms
Title: "How are you feeling today?"
Purpose: Capture the patient's immediate health state, symptoms, and subjective wellbeing indicators.

Text Fields
Field	Type	Description
Current Health Concerns	Long text	Free-form description of symptoms, discomfort, and health challenges
Interactive Sliders (0–10 scale)
Slider	Scale Description	Labels
Physical Comfort Level	Pain scale	0 = No Discomfort → 10 = Severe Pain
Energy Level	Vitality	0 = Depleted → 10 = High Energy
Sleep Quality	Rest quality	0 = Very Restless → 10 = Deeply Restorative
Stress Level	Mental load	0 = Very Calm → 10 = Overwhelmed
Slider Interpretations:

Pain: 0–3 = Low, 4–6 = Moderate, 7–10 = Severe
Energy: 0–3 = Low, 4–6 = Moderate, 7–8 = Good, 9–10 = High
Sleep: 0–3 = Poor, 4–6 = Fair, 7–8 = Good, 9–10 = Excellent
Stress: 0–3 = Low, 4–6 = Moderate, 7–8 = High, 9–10 = Very High
Step 3 — Medical History
Title: "Your Health Journey"
Purpose: Understand ongoing treatments and contraindications that may influence the TCM diagnosis.

Field	Type	Description
Current Medications & Supplements	Long text	Lists all medications, herbs, supplements and their purpose
Allergies & Sensitivities	Long text	Food, medication, and environmental allergies

Title: Vitals
Purpose: Capture objective clinical measurements that inform the diagnosis.

Field	Type	Example Input	Notes
Blood Pressure	Text	120/80	Optional; if known
Heart Rate	Text	72	Beats per minute; optional
Temperature	Text	98.6°F	Optional; if known
Weight	Text	Your weight	Optional
Height	Text	Your height	Optional
Step 5 — TCM Assessment
Title: "Energy & Balance"
Purpose: The core TCM diagnostic section. Captures tongue examination, pulse quality, and constitutional body type — the primary tools of Traditional Chinese Medicine diagnosis.

5a. Body Constitution
Field	Type	Description
Body Constitution	Long text	Patient describes temperature preferences, thirst patterns, preference for warm or cold — used to identify Yin/Yang balance
Prompt: "Do you tend to feel cold or hot? Are you often thirsty? Do you prefer warm or cold drinks?"

5b. Tongue Examination
Patients can either upload a tongue photo or manually select characteristics:

Photo Upload:

Drag & drop or click to upload
Accepts all image formats
Files stored securely on the server
Manual Tongue Characteristics (Dropdowns):

Field	Options
Tongue Color	Pale / Normal pink / Red / Dark red / Purple
Tongue Coating	Thin white / Thick white / Yellow / Gray / Black
Tongue Shape	Normal / Swollen / Thin / Stiff / Flaccid
Tongue Moisture	Normal / Dry / Wet / Sticky
Additional Observations	Free text for any other tongue notes
5c. Pulse Diagnosis
Field	Type	Description
Pulse Quality	Long text	Patient or practitioner describes pulse character (strong, weak, fast, slow, irregular, wiry, slippery, etc.)
Step 6 — Lifestyle & Harmony
Title: "Lifestyle & Harmony"
Purpose: Understand daily habits that affect the flow of Qi (vital energy) and overall wellbeing.

Field	Label	Prompt
Diet / Nourishment Patterns	Long text	Typical eating patterns, food preferences, dietary approaches
Movement Practices	Long text	Exercise routine, yoga, movement activities and frequency
Life Balance	Long text	Work-life balance, stress management, relaxation techniques, spiritual practices
Post-Submission Flow
Once the form is submitted:

Assessment is saved to the pending queue with status: pending
Practitioner receives notification in the Admin Portal
Practitioner reviews data, selects an I Ching Hexagram (1–64) with moving lines
Practitioner adds SOAP notes (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan)
System generates AI-powered report combining all data
Assessment is marked completed
Report is available for review and delivery to patient
