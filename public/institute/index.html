A three-course pathway

Keep following the questions.

The catalog describes a wider learning path across foundational theory, the psyche and diagnostic reasoning. The Foundations class is active now; the remaining pathway is in development.

COURSE 01 ACTIVE CLASS

Foundations of Chinese Medicine

Explore the philosophical roots and core frameworks of Chinese medicine, with attention to balance, change and the relationship between people and their environments.

Topics include

  • History, philosophy, Yin and Yang
  • The Five Elements and their relationships
  • Jing, Qi, Shen, Blood and body fluids
  • Qi transformation and Zang-Fu organ theory
  • Foundations of health, disease and prevention

Current Classroom topics move from Yin-Yang and Five Element foundations through vital substances, Qi transformation and internal organ functions.

COURSE 02 CATALOG PATHWAY

The Psyche in Chinese Medicine

Study traditional perspectives on consciousness, emotion and physical wellbeing, including the Five Spirits and their relationships to the organs.

Topics include

  • Shen, Hun, Po, Yi and Zhi
  • The Seven Emotions and Qi
  • Emotional patterns and organ relationships
  • Dialogue with contemporary psychology

Planned course outline; schedule and availability have not been announced.

COURSE 03 CATALOG PATHWAY

Advanced Diagnosis & Treatment

Build foundational clinical reasoning through traditional diagnostic frameworks, organized observation and guided case analysis.

Topics include

  • Eight Principles and pattern differentiation
  • Qi, Blood, fluids and Zang-Fu patterns
  • Channels and introductory tongue and pulse concepts
  • Treatment principles and case analysis

Planned course outline; practical training is subject to separate arrangements and appropriate supervision.
