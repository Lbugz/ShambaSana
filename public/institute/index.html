Who it’s for: Adult learners from diverse educational, professional and cultural backgrounds who are interested in Chinese medicine and wellbeing. Foundations introduces the core ideas; the later courses require completion of the preceding courses.

Online classes combine lesson presentations, assigned reading and live discussion. Google Classroom organizes course materials and assignments. Recording availability will be confirmed for each cohort. Learning activities connect foundational ideas with everyday observations and carefully framed case examples.

Meet your instructor

Experience, care
and curiosity.

Dumisani Kambi-Shamba

LAc, MSTOM · Founder & Instructor

Dumisani brings decades of study and practice in Chinese medicine, herbal traditions, and nutrition to the Shamba Sana Institute. Through his work with LifeRx, he has emphasized prevention, education, and understanding each person within their wider environment.

His approach centers on humility, service, and sharing knowledge—helping students explore traditional ideas through thoughtful questions and careful observation.
