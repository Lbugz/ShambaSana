Meet your instructor

Experience, care
and curiosity.

Dumisani Kambi-Shamba

LAc, MSTOM · Founder & Instructor

Dumisani brings decades of study and practice in Chinese medicine, herbal traditions, and nutrition to the Shamba Sana Institute. Through his work with LifeRx, he has emphasized prevention, education, and understanding each person within their wider environment.

His approach centers on humility, service, and sharing knowledge—helping students explore traditional ideas through thoughtful questions and careful observation.
