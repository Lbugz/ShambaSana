How online
learning works.

Who it’s for: Adult learners interested in Chinese medicine and wellbeing. Foundations starts with the core ideas, so no previous study is required for Semester 1.

Live study: Join online lessons and discussion, prepare with assigned reading, and explore educational case examples. Course materials, assignments and available recordings are organized in Google Classroom.

Joining after the start: Foundations Semester 1 offers reviewed self-paced catch-up. Staff confirm the materials, support, access period and completion plan with you before payment.

Learning and assessment: Quizzes, written work, discussion and case analysis help you connect the ideas. Your semester syllabus sets the attendance, grading and completion requirements. Read the study expectations →

Meet your instructor

Your instructor.

Dumisani Kambi-Shamba

LAc, MSTOM · Founder & Instructor

Dumisani brings decades of study and practice in Chinese medicine, herbal traditions, and nutrition to the Shamba Sana Institute. Through his work with LifeRx, he has emphasized prevention, education, and understanding each person within their wider environment.

His approach centers on humility, service, and sharing knowledge—helping students explore traditional ideas through thoughtful questions and careful observation.
