Shamba Sana Institute

Registration privacy.

How the Institute uses information submitted through its course registration form.

What you share

The form collects your name, email, chosen course, semester, study format, previous-study status and any payment-plan choice and consent. Your device’s time zone is detected to help coordinate classes. Phone number, cohort details and a message are optional. Please do not include health information or private documents.

How it is used

The Institute uses these details to review preparation, contact you about admission, manage semester enrollment and coordinate payment and class access. Authorized staff review registrations through the Shamba Sana platform. Submitting the form does not create a public profile.

Payment and class access

When checkout is available, card details are entered on Stripe’s hosted checkout. Accepted students may receive emails and Google Classroom invitations through the Institute’s Google account. Sandbox checkout collects no real tuition and grants no real class access. A monthly sandbox plan saves the test payment method with Stripe for the agreed finite installments; card details are not collected on this form. Final live enrollment and payment terms are pending.

Questions or corrections

Contact onlineclass@liferx.us to ask about your registration, correct details or request removal of a registration record. Staff will review your request and explain any enrollment or recordkeeping requirements that apply. This page concerns Institute registration; Integrative Care has separate privacy information.
