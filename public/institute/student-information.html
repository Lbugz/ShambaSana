Begin with Foundations, then progress to the Psyche and Advanced Diagnosis & Treatment. Foundations has three semesters; its Semesters 2 and 3 are part of Course I.

Study: Shen, Hun, Po, Yi and Zhi; the Seven Emotions; emotional-Qi and organ relationships; sleep and introductory case interpretation.

Outcome: Compare traditional frameworks of mind and emotion, explain their relationships and recognize when further qualified assessment is needed. These frameworks are not presented as psychiatric diagnoses.

Study: Eight Principles, Qi–Blood–Fluids, organ and Five Element patterns, channels, Six Stages, Four Levels, Three Burners, and introductory tongue and pulse concepts.

Outcome: Organize fictional case information, distinguish traditional patterns and explain conceptual treatment strategies while recognizing the need for qualified assessment.

Joining or continuing

Adult learners from all backgrounds are welcome. You may apply for a later course while completing earlier study. Share whether each prerequisite is completed, in progress or registered; staff confirm eligibility before admission. Completing Foundations Semester 1 alone does not establish eligibility for Course II.

Know the commitment

Tuition & refunds

Foundations tuition$2,000 per semester

Three semesters · $6,000 total
Semester 1 self-paced catch-up is also $2,000.

Each semester needs a new enrollment and payment choice. There are no automatic next-semester charges. Course II and III tuition and semester structure remain pending. Required books, supplies and additional fees are disclosed before payment.

Payment options · Sandbox preview

Foundations registration previews a $2,000 full payment or four monthly payments of $500. The monthly plan collects $500 at checkout and three further monthly installments, then stops. Late-start plans may extend beyond class dates. These draft options collect no real tuition; final live payment and withdrawal terms must be confirmed before launch.

Withdrawals & refunds
  • Before a semester begins: withdraw for a full refund of that semester’s tuition.
  • After instruction starts: refunds are based on unused scheduled instruction.
  • If the Institute cancels before a semester: that semester’s tuition is refunded.

Your enrollment agreement will specify dates and calculations, including self-paced withdrawal, transfers, unmet prerequisites and prepaid future semesters. Applicable statutory rights remain in effect.

Request withdrawal at onlineclass@liferx.us. These principles do not replace your final enrollment agreement.

Prepare for class

How online study works

Live online

Join lesson presentations and discussion, complete reading, and explore educational cases. You’ll need a suitable device, reliable internet and your course materials.

Self-paced catch-up

Currently available for Foundations Semester 1, subject to review. Staff agree your learning plan, support, access period and completion requirements with you.

Materials, recordings & class access

Google Classroom organizes course materials and assignments. Current classrooms contain recordings and study resources; included materials and access periods are confirmed for your semester. Other self-paced offerings have not been announced.

Class access follows admission and the required enrollment steps. Keep your confirmation and orientation information for joining instructions.

Attendance & assessment

Prepare for class, arrive on time and participate respectfully. Assessment may include quizzes, written exams, reading, participation, presentations and case analysis.

Available syllabi allow two absences without penalty and missed written exams to be made up within one week by arrangement. Your confirmed semester syllabus sets the applicable attendance rules, grading thresholds, deadlines and self-paced requirements.

Learn with care

Completion & conduct

Program completion requires all three core courses, passing grades, required assessments, attendance and applicable administrative requirements. Final thresholds and the certificate title will be set out in the approved handbook.

A completion certificate records educational achievement. It does not confer professional licensure or authority to diagnose, prescribe, perform acupuncture or provide independent clinical care.

A respectful learning community
  • Respect classmates and instructors across cultural, religious and philosophical differences.
  • Submit original work, acknowledge sources and follow the instructor’s guidance on AI-assisted assignments.
  • Use fictional or appropriately anonymized cases. Do not share identifiable health information in class or registration messages.
  • Ask permission before recording or sharing classes and materials.
  • Work within the boundaries of educational cases and your preparation.

Discuss routine learning concerns with your instructor. Raise safety, harassment or misconduct concerns directly with administration through onlineclass@liferx.us. The final handbook will describe complaint review and disciplinary procedures.
