Course I — Foundations of Chinese Medicine · Fall 2026

Three semesters · $2,000 per semester ($6,000 total). No previous study is required for Semester 1. The live cohort runs September 9–December 11, 2026, Wednesdays and Fridays, with times TBD in Eastern Time (America/New_York, EST/EDT). Semester 2 and 3 also meet Wednesdays and Fridays; dates, times and teaching hours will be confirmed. Register for self-paced Semester 1 catch-up; materials, support and completion arrangements are confirmed before payment.

Explore Chinese medical thought, Daoist perspectives, Yin–Yang, Five Elements, Three Treasures, Qi, Blood, body fluids, Jing, Shen, Zang-Fu organ theory and introductory channel concepts.

Learning outcome: Explain central theories and describe relationships between organs, elements and vital substances using introductory educational examples.

Course II — The Psyche in Chinese Medicine · Proposed Spring 2027

Foundations preparation required. Staff review eligibility; tuition and semester structure are awaiting confirmation.

Study Shen, Hun, Po, Yi and Zhi; the Seven Emotions; emotional-Qi and organ relationships; sleep and emotional experiences; and introductory psycho-emotional case interpretation.

Learning outcome: Compare traditional frameworks of mind and emotion, explain their relationships and recognize when further qualified assessment is appropriate. Traditional patterns are not presented as psychiatric diagnoses.

Core reading: Giovanni Maciocia, The Psyche in Chinese Medicine. The required edition and final reading list will be confirmed before class.

Register for Spring 2027 review →

Course III — Advanced Diagnosis & Treatment · Proposed Summer 2027

Foundations and Psyche preparation required. Staff review eligibility; tuition and semester structure are awaiting confirmation.

Explore the Eight Principles, Qi–Blood–Fluids, organ patterns, Five Elements, channels, Six Stages, Four Levels, Three Burners and introductory tongue and pulse concepts.

Learning outcome: Organize fictional case information, distinguish traditional patterns, explain conceptual treatment strategies and identify situations requiring qualified assessment.

Case exercises are educational. This virtual course does not include independent patient treatment or practical workshops.

Preparation & admission.

We welcome adult learners from diverse educational, professional and cultural backgrounds who are willing to complete readings and assessments, participate thoughtfully and respect the learning community.

You may register for a later course while progressing through the earlier courses. Tell us whether you have completed, are studying or have registered for each prerequisite. The Institute reviews this information against its records; staff confirm preparation requirements before your selected semester starts.

  1. Select your course, semester and study format in the separate registration form.
  2. Provide your contact details, prerequisite status and earlier cohort information.
  3. The Institute reviews your preparation and confirms any outstanding steps.
  4. Review the confirmed schedule, final handbook, financial policies and enrollment agreement.
  5. Complete the agreed tuition payment after acceptance and review.
  6. Receive enrollment confirmation, orientation information and class access once the required steps are complete.

Registration is not automatic admission. Payment does not replace prerequisite review or completion.

Tuition & financial information.

Foundations: three semesters at $2,000 each ($6,000 total). Enroll and pay separately for each semester. Semester 1 self-paced catch-up is also $2,000. Course II and III tuition and semester structure are awaiting confirmation. Electives and workshops are priced separately.

After review, you receive a separate semester confirmation and private payment invitation. Continuing requires your choice and a new payment; completion never authorizes automatic charging. Installments within a semester require separate written approval.

Required textbooks, supplies and any additional fees will be disclosed before payment. Scholarship, tuition assistance and work-study arrangements are not currently published offers.

Refund principles

Withdraw before your semester begins for a full refund of that semester’s tuition. After instruction begins, refunds are based on unused scheduled instruction. Prepaid future semesters, transfers, unmet prerequisites and self-paced withdrawal calculations will be specified before payment. If the Institute cancels before a semester begins, its tuition is refunded. Applicable statutory rights remain in effect.

Request withdrawal at onlineclass@liferx.us. Your enrollment agreement will include the specific dates and calculation; this overview is not a checkout agreement.

How online study works.

Virtual learning

Prepare for online classes with a reliable internet connection, a suitable device and access to the course materials. Live discussion, lesson presentations, reading and educational case examples support learning. Current classrooms contain recordings and study resources; included materials and access periods are confirmed for your semester. Self-paced catch-up registration is available for Foundations Semester 1, subject to staff review. Other self-paced offerings have not been announced.

Assessment

Learning may be assessed through quizzes, written examinations, reading assignments, participation, presentations and case analysis. The syllabus will specify assessment methods, grading thresholds and deadlines.

Attendance

Regular attendance and preparation matter. Attend on time, complete assigned reading and participate respectfully. Available syllabi allow two absences without penalty and missed written examinations to be made up within one week by arrangement. The confirmed semester syllabus will state applicable attendance and self-paced requirements.

Support & access

Raise learning difficulties or accommodation needs with the Institute before your course begins where possible. Contact onlineclass@liferx.us for course support, admissions questions or to request an accommodation discussion. Avoid sending personal health information by email.

Completion & certificates.

The program’s completion pathway requires all three core courses, passing grades, required academic assessments, attendance and applicable administrative requirements. Exact thresholds and the final certificate title will be confirmed in the approved handbook.

A completion certificate records educational achievement. This program does not itself confer professional licensure or authority to diagnose, prescribe, perform acupuncture or provide independent clinical care. Electives and workshops do not replace the required core courses.

A respectful learning community.

  • Treat classmates and instructors with dignity and respect across cultural, religious and philosophical differences.
  • Submit original work, acknowledge sources and follow the instructor’s guidance on AI-assisted assignments.
  • Protect confidentiality. Use fictional or appropriately anonymized cases and avoid sharing identifiable health information in class or registration messages.
  • Ask permission before recording or sharing classes and learning materials.
  • Recognize the boundaries of educational case studies and your level of preparation.

Discuss routine learning concerns with your instructor where appropriate. Serious safety, harassment or misconduct concerns should be raised directly with administration. Email onlineclass@liferx.us to request contact with administration. The final handbook will describe complaint review and disciplinary procedures.
