The founding idea

The House of Life,
reimagined.

LEARN / REST / GROW / CONNECT

The ancient Egyptian Per Ankh—the House of Life—brought scholarship, healing and knowledge preservation together. That idea inspires our vision for contemporary community life.

We believe wellbeing grows through what we learn, the relationships we nurture and the environments we share. Shamba Sana connects those foundations through education, thoughtful practice and a lasting commitment to community.

Our longer-term ambition is a welcoming urban sanctuary: a place to share knowledge, grow food, gather and find restoration. We are taking our first steps online.

Wellbeing belongs in community

We welcome people from all backgrounds, with a particular commitment to communities historically underserved by wellness institutions. Our future spaces and programs will be shaped with community participation, dignity and access in mind.
