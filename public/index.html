The founding idea

The House of Life,
reimagined.

LEARN / REST / GROW / CONNECT

Inspired by the ancient Egyptian concept of Per Ankh—the House of Life—Shamba Sana imagines a place where learning, care, nature and community belong together.

Our long-term vision is a welcoming urban sanctuary: a place to learn, connect with practitioners, grow food, preserve knowledge and find restoration.

Our first steps are online learning through the Institute and a developing Integrative Care pilot. Together, they bring education and practitioner support into reach while the physical sanctuary remains a future ambition.

Rooted in community

We aim to bring knowledge, restorative environments and practitioner support closer to communities historically underserved by wellness institutions. Community participation and respectful stewardship of ancestral knowledge guide the vision we are building.

A vision for the spaces we share

Our longer-term vision brings learning, care, nourishing food, nature and community together in welcoming urban sanctuaries. Community-informed research and responsible knowledge preservation would help us learn from that work and share its benefits.
