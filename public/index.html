The founding idea

The House of Life,
reimagined.

LEARN / REST / GROW / CONNECT

Inspired by the ancient Egyptian concept of Per Ankh—the House of Life—Shamba Sana imagines a place where learning, care, nature and community belong together.

Our long-term vision is a welcoming urban sanctuary: a place to learn, grow food, preserve knowledge and find restoration, with access for communities underserved by wellness institutions. Community participation and respectful stewardship of ancestral knowledge guide that work.

We are beginning online, with the Institute and a developing Integrative Care service. The physical sanctuary remains a future ambition.
